Planting guide

From scent to sprout.

Thank you for taking your first step toward a greener drive! When your wooden car diffuser is ready for a fresh scent, don't toss the paper. Give it a second life in soil and watch it grow.

Skip to planting

Part 1 · Removing your seed paper

  1. 01

    Unhook or unclip your wooden box

    Before opening your reusable wooden diffuser, remove it from your car so the paper slips out cleanly. (For the hanging edition, lift the cord off your rearview mirror; for the vent clip edition, gently pull it straight off the air vent.)

  2. 02

    Open the wooden diffuser box

    Hold the wooden box flat in your palm and carefully slide or lift open the lid. Your wooden box is crafted to be reused again and again, so set the lid aside safely while you swap your insert.

  3. 03

    Lift out & refresh

    Gently lift out the spent seed paper insert by its edges so you don't tear the embedded seeds. Slide a fresh seed paper refill inside, close your wooden box, and return it to your drive.

Part 2 · Planting your seed paper

  1. 04

    Prepare your pot & soil

    Fill a planting pot, such as the Seed Scent terracotta pot from The Bloom or The Garden bundle, with loose, well-draining potting soil, leaving about half an inch of space below the rim. Lightly moisten the soil before planting.

  2. 05

    Lay & lightly cover the seed paper

    Place the seed paper flat on top of the damp soil (you can tear it into smaller pieces if using a smaller pot). Gently pat a thin layer, about 1/8 inch, of loose soil over the paper so the seeds stay snug without being buried too deeply.

  3. 06

    Water, sunshine & patience

    Mist or water gently so the paper and top soil stay evenly damp, never soggy, for the first 10 to 14 days while seeds germinate. Place your pot in a warm, sunny spot and watch your first sprouts appear!

Quick sprout care reference

Care factorFirst 2 weeks (germination)After sprouting (growth)
Watering Keep seed paper continuously moist with gentle misting or light watering. Water whenever the top inch of soil feels dry to the touch.
Sunlight Bright, warm indirect light or gentle morning sun. 4 to 6+ hours of daily direct sunlight on a sunny windowsill or patio.
Soil depth Cover with only a light dusting (1/8 inch) of soil so sprouts reach light easily. Roots anchor naturally as the biodegradable paper breaks down in soil.
