Seed Scent · A plantable car air freshener

The air freshener that doesn't end when the scent does.

A wooden holder, a disc of seed paper, natural essential oils and a plant-based fixative. Hang it, breathe it, then plant the paper.

  1. 01 · Hang it

    Loop it over the mirror. The holder is wood, the disc is seed paper, and it swings when you corner.

  2. 02 · Breathe

    Natural essential oils, held in the paper by a plant-based fixative so the scent lasts scent duration.

  3. 03 · It fades

    Every air freshener fades. Most go in the trash. This one is just getting started.

  4. 04 · Plant it

    Pop the disc out of the holder, tear it up, press it into soil and water it. The paper breaks down and the seeds take over. The holder stays in the car. tbc

How to plant it.

Steps are a draft until the team confirms them

  1. 01

    Tear the paper into pieces about the size of a coin.

  2. 02

    Press them into a pot or a patch of soil, no deeper than a fingertip.

  3. 03

    Water well and keep the soil damp.

  4. 04

    Give it sun and wait for the first sprouts.
